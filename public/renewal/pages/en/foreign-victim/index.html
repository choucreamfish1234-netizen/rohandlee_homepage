Questions before consulting

What information should I prepare?

Please share a timeline, messages, contracts, payment records and any notices from the police or court. Keep the original files and tell us about any upcoming deadlines.

Can I contact you before gathering every document?

Yes. Explain the incident and what stage it has reached. We can first discuss which information and documents are needed.

Is a translated page a promise of direct consultation in that language?

No. Please specify your preferred language when contacting us. Interpretation arrangements, availability and any fees will be confirmed before consultation.

Tell us what happened.

Share the circumstances of the incident and its current stage. We will discuss the next steps with you.
